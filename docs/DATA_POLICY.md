## Local historical measurements

The source workbooks/PDF and extracted observations are kept local while redistribution terms are unverified. They include Lahore Irrigation Zone measurements, filtered to Lahore District. Source fields may contain private addresses or names.

Exclude original archives, raw and processed readings, per-well exports and source-row audit files from the public release until permission is established. Do not assume removing names automatically makes the underlying dataset redistributable.

## NASA and other third-party products

Retain provider/product attribution, parameter units, periods and metadata in local research outputs. Document how to retrieve NASA POWER data and follow the provider's terms. Download caches are excluded from the initial release. The code license does not replace NASA or another provider's applicable terms.

GRACE is optional regional context; it is not a local well-depth measurement. A blank questionnaire is a template, not collected evidence.

## Public outputs

Review aggregate metrics and figures before publication. A plot, notebook cell or report can still expose source readings, a person's identity or restricted information.

Synthetic fixtures should be unmistakably fictional and must not be reconstructed private source rows. Never describe synthetic data as observations.

## Scope and interpretation

The documented model evaluation uses historical data ending in pre-monsoon 2020. It does not establish current groundwater levels, coverage of every Lahore neighborhood, drinking-water safety or proven causes of decline.

Concern classes describe historical monitored-well trends. Straight-line 2030 scenarios are conditional extrapolations, not validated forecasts.

## Maintainer review

Review the actual staged files and previous Git commits. Ignore rules do not remove tracked content or earlier history. If restricted content is already in history, resolve the issue before public release; do not silently rewrite shared history.
