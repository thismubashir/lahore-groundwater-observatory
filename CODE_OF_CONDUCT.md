# Community conduct

Treat contributors and data providers respectfully. Discuss evidence and code rather than attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Give constructive feedback, acknowledge uncertainty and credit others' work. Never pressure someone to disclose restricted observations or personal survey records.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate public content, close discussions, or restrict participation proportionately. Concerns may be raised through a maintainer contact explicitly listed in the repository. If none is available, do not post private allegations, identifying information or security-sensitive details in a public issue.

This is a project-specific conduct policy, not an adoption of an external branded code-of-conduct version.
